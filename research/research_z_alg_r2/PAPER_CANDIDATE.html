Working title

Determinantal Quotient Algebras for Enumerating Real Materials in Nonlinear Maxwell Scattering

Scope: one complex receiver, s independent illuminations, 2s independent real material cells, fixed finite scalar-TM Green operators, and an invertible leading determinantal block. Four- and six-material instances have been implemented; larger cases remain untested. A larger known background can be eliminated exactly, but is not counted as additional unknown material coverage.

Scientific problem

Denominator clearing introduces algebraic base loci, while finite-budget local optimization can miss material roots. Can shared real-material compatibility expose an explicitly computable quotient algebra that enumerates candidates without material initial guesses and retains full nonlinear physical validation?

Two candidate originality claims

  1. A conditional physical determinantal reduction with an explicit quotient basis. Balanced acquisition yields paired bilinear compatibility; an invertible leading block gives a quotient of length binom(2s,s), with all monomials of degree≤s as basis. Conjugate-swap pairing guarantees finite unique eliminated-coordinate lifts. Nodes, poles, multiplicity, reference dependence and a non-necessity counterexample are explicit. Support: MATHEMATICAL_THEORY A–F and theory/general_quotient.md. Mathematically supported under assumptions; specific application priority HOLD. Eagon–Northcott, Hilbert–Burch, CSI/SOM, determinantal degrees and eigenvalue root extraction are established tools.
  2. A runnable enumerator with scoped fresh-data evidence. Four-material Q6 matches strong controls at lower measured cost. Six-material Q20 returns2/1/1 physical roots on three fresh blinded observations versus1/1/1 for frozen64-start reduced Full-J. Both sets contain GT; the extra material differs by1.15879 RMS and passes fresh full-forward verification. Single-batch total wall is0.1044s vs1.5180s, including fixed preparation and candidate validation. Finite experimental support; not superiority over every optimizer or a certified global coverage theorem.

The48-cell SVD/Full-J blind imaging study demonstrates practical reconstruction and a conditioning/noise failure. It is not an originality claim and is not48-variable quotient enumeration.

Proposed paper structure

  1. Nonlinear finite wave inversion and denominator-base-locus costs.
  2. Fixed physical kernels and shared-real-material compatibility.
  3. Balanced determinantal quotient theorem, finite lift, and degeneration counterexample.
  4. Explicit quotient multiplication operators, numerical qualification and physical filtering.
  5. Four- and six-material blind enumeration, strong fixed-kernel controls, cost and negative evidence.
  6. Spatial blind imaging as a separate standard-tool application; unresolved scaling and certification.

Core figures: general6_blind_roots; algebraic_mechanism; quotient_fresh_holdout. HIGHFREQ48 imaging is a separate application/limitation figure.

Claims explicitly excluded

  • First polynomial wave inversion, first homotopy, first current lifting, first determinantal quotient, or first eigenvalue polynomial solver.
  • All ordinary methods miss the additional root: only the frozen64-start protocol missed it. A different budget or specialized algebraic solver may recover it.
  • Certified global uniqueness, all physical connected components, physical symmetry group, computed Galois/monodromy group, Full66 or48-variable algebraic coverage.
  • General noisy root enumeration, continuous-PDE or3D vector-Maxwell validity, unknown geometry, tensor material, or arbitrary nonlinear decoder generalization.
  • Scientific acceptance or established novelty merely from source publication and readable webpages.

Most important unfinished work

Compare against specialized bilinear/determinantal solvers with the same physical kernel and coefficient representation, and determine whether the physical compression yields a reproducible advantage beyond generic structured elimination. The most consequential mathematical failure boundary is T degeneration: build an alternative chart/representation that handles it without converting numerical failure into a false uniqueness claim. Strict coefficient/root certification is separately necessary for any certified completeness statement.

Current judgment

The result now extends beyond the original four-material special case and supports a scoped methods-paper candidate. A broad, general-Maxwell breakthrough remains HOLD. Direct priority against Everett and existing structured polynomial algorithms is unresolved; the finite search audit does not settle it.
