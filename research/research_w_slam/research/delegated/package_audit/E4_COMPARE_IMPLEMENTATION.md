# E4 finite-prior comparison implementation

Implemented `tools/e4_compare.py`; not run. No solver imports or physical calls; parent must execute through the shared E4 CPU wrapper. No changes to inference or fitting.

Inputs are the completed `results/e2e3/finite_bank/online/{FINITE_GRID,FINITE_BANK,OBSERVATIONS}.npz`, the online bank cost receipt, and `results/e2e3/inference/POSTERIORS.npz` with (parent,repeat,time,atom) weights/log-likelihoods and matching parent/repeat ID vectors. Only the final posterior is compared. A normalization identity explicitly determines whether stored likelihood entries are cumulative or incremental; only the interpretation reproducing the reference may be sampled. Inconsistent inputs fail sampling rather than silently multiplying data twice.

Comparators are MAP point; product of ten exact coordinate marginal masses restricted/renormalized on legal finite atoms; occupancy mixture preserving exact support masses and approximating each conditional by a moment Gaussian kernel; global ten-coordinate moment Gaussian kernel; existing corrected tempered SMC and prior-proposal importance sampling, each 256/1024 particles and seeds 0/1/2. Projection parameters come from the exact reference and are labeled OFFLINE_PROJECTION_ONLY, not deployable. Gaussian kernels are normalized against the declared finite (conditional) prior masses; moments after discrete truncation need not equal the fitted parameters. Empty-support mixture components integrate unused wall-material coordinates and use pose only. Any required covariance not strictly SPD is NOT_DEFINED; no jitter or pseudoinverse repair.

Target-coordinate CDF, event and important-support mass errors are compared separately to .02; square task regret to .05 reference Bayes risk + .005². All-ten-coordinate CDF errors are also retained independently. Important modes use the existing API's reference mass >=.05 convention. Row checks describe declared tolerances, not a new scientific gate.

Outputs under `results/e4/comparison/`: incremental `RESULTS.json`, complete `FULL_TABLE.csv`, `WEIGHTS.npz` (undefined rows have explicitly labeled NaN weight vectors), `REFERENCE_FINAL.npz`, and an INCONCLUSIVE `gate.json` reserved for parent interpretation. All failures, NOT_DEFINED and budget-skipped rows count in full method denominators. Shared E2E3 bank setup attribution is copied, not described as free or recharged as E4 physics. Material 3×3 nodes and pose 5×5 nodes define this discrete prior; they are not accuracy estimates of continuous integration.
