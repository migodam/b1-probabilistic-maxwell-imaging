## Issues that materially affect interpretation

1. Neither the archived prose nor registry statuses establish literature originality. CIE/Dual/FFT, finite resolvents, material poles, MM and current-to-material splitting have concrete prior-art counterparts.
2. LUCIE is a direct material-readout comparator. Its authors' institutional abstract verifies a two-stage current/support estimate then a field-based regularized linear contrast solve, with synthetic and experimental tests. Treat it as more than an unimplemented idea.
3. The original literature inputs do not provide an exact Fresnel pixel GT or interior horn field. The actual raw files and 2001 primary descriptor were independently obtained. Current R7 measured inversion is exploratory and negatively distinguishes fit from recovery: all arms have identical final endpoints, substantial withheld error and box-saturated contrast.
4. P21 and P22 bibliography labels describe datasets. Manuscript references must use actual primary descriptor titles/authors and DOI-resolved metadata. Optional P11 issue date is after the task date; distinguish preprint/early-online from future issue date. No external claim is made here for optional unverified entries.
5. The inherited 2D disk-pulse model is not a vector 3D model. Small algebraic residuals do not establish physical discretization or calibrated material recovery.
