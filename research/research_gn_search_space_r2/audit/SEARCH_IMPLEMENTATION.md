# Search implementation and accounting

This study imports the historical `research_z_alg_r4_diversity/src/physics.py` `Model` and `Regularizer` without changing those files. The physical model remains the full 576-cell, 12-source, 48-receiver scalar-TM VIE. The search consumes only released operators, observations and saved Native-TRF material endpoints. It never imports truth or offline score code.

E2 has 12 low-contrast clean Native-TRF anchors and smooth/TV coefficients `(1e-4,1e-4)` times 1, 0.1, and 0. Each task has 45 seconds, 500 LU and 30,000 complex RHS. The original anchor is included. The common objective is the original normalized nonlinear data objective plus the specified regularizer. Exact dense TRF uses the original analytic residual/Jacobian, bounds 0.02..20, `x_scale=jac`, and the historical stopping tolerances `(ftol,xtol,gtol)=(1e-10,1e-10,1e-8)`. A genuine successful early stop ends that task.

E3 has 24 clean/noisy low-contrast anchors and four arms: ordinary continuation, two-band Haar directions, Euclidean Green pullback, and soft normalized material-Jacobian filtering. The two full-pixel Haar details are `m-blockmean2(m)` and `blockmean2(m)-blockmean4(m)`. Each band supplies both signs at prescribed unfiltered RMS radii 0.03 and 0.1. Green and soft filtering never renormalize the filtered result. Box feasibility multiplies the entire direction by one scalar. It does not clip coordinates. At an active pixel this scalar can be zero: such seeds remain part of the protocol and are exposed in score tables. For shortened positive scalars, scalar ULP steps toward zero continue until the computed endpoint obeys the box; this never changes direction. Earlier roundoff failures remain in receipts.

The soft filter uses `K=J_real/||Y||`, retained right singular vectors, and `lambda=(epsilon/(radius*sqrt(576)))^2`, epsilon=1e-4 clean or 1e-3 noisy. The filtered direction is `V diag(lambda/(s^2+lambda)) V^T p`. Green geometry comes from `src/space.py`; only its Euclidean orthonormal `approx_basis` is used for proposal projection. Each seed gets at most 3 seconds or 8 function evaluations of the same TRF. All evaluations, including rejected proposals, failed correctors and anchor preparation, consume the arm's single budget. The best observed common objective then receives a remaining-budget continuation. Selection never sees truth.

The task timer starts before `Model` construction. Full anchor-Jacobian spectrum preparation is timed and counted for E3 and full-regularization E2 (which is exactly reused as E3 clean continuation). Public observation and immutable operator file reads happen before this timer; this small public preload is common. The approved plan reuses the preceding fixed G/S operator spectra as immutable offline setup. Their file load and newly constructed current-complement orthogonal basis are timed in each arm. No target-dependent J or material geometry cache is reused. The Green tangent inverse, current geometry and J preparation are performed within each Green arm. Model operation counts and wall timings are retained. The full-regularization E2 predictions reused in E3 have zero incremental compute, with the original charged receipt preserved. They must not be counted as an independent repeat.

Candidate NPZs preserve anchor, top1, raw directions, filtered feasible directions, before/after materials, and anchor normalized-J right vectors/singular values where applicable. Arrays are saved progressively if a budget exception interrupts the eight-seed loop. Scalar receipt traces cover every physical evaluation; correction attempt rows cover successes, failed attempts and stopping reasons. The reconstruction minimum is an optimizer-candidate minimum and supplies no independent-root evidence.

Several early E3 correctors failed because scalar-ray endpoint addition exceeded a bound by rounding; all remain recorded, with no replacement or free correction. Scalar-only arithmetic was then adjusted as above, without changing the ray protocol. A multi-task native process later died while processing digit4 Green. Completed receipts were kept; a separate `results/e3/PROCESS_FAILURES.json` records the lost partial attempt with UNKNOWN costs. Fresh processes per task isolate native state. Geometry numerical driver changes are recorded by its owner; no physical model or scientific budget changed.

After observing genuine 1–6-Jacobian stops, the parent approved one separately labeled stopping diagnostic: curved_bar, digit2, digit7 and ring; all three weights; the same physical/time/LU/RHS budgets; `(ftol,xtol,gtol)=(1e-12,1e-12,1e-12)`. These tasks have `_tight` identifiers and never replace main predictions. A separate public-only endpoint-stationarity diagnostic records analytic full gradients, projected gradients and active counts, with additional forward/J costs charged separately; it cannot update any prediction.

Formal scoring is a separate process in `src/score_search.py`, invoked only after `ALL_SEARCH_PREDICTIONS_SEALED.json` closes all planned task dispositions. It reads the released historical truth, computes original RMSE/amplitude, MAE and reconstruction-only Otsu IoU, and exposes online top1 versus offline bestK. BestK includes anchor, finite seeds/corrections and selected top1 and is labeled OFFLINE throughout. It performs zero new physical solves.

## Closed execution

All 144 planned predictions are closed, finite, sealed and independently scored: 36 frozen E2, 96 frozen E3, and 12 separately labeled tighter-stopping diagnostics. Twelve clean E3 continuation predictions exactly reuse E2 full-regularization runs, giving 132 unique successful physical runs. No successful task reached its scientific time/LU/RHS cap. Main E3 maximum wall time was 10.5395 s; tighter-stop maximum wall time was 15.4150 s. The separate public-only stationarity pass used 36 extra LU and 2,160 complex RHS. Its full gradients, projected gradients, active counts and SciPy-scaled optimality are preserved. All frozen E2 endpoints meet the historical scaled gradient tolerance (largest scaled optimality below 9.53e-9).

`results/e3/COMPLETION.json` contains sums excluding duplicate reuse, observed task durations, and the explicit UNKNOWN partial native-process cost. `SCORES.json` files retain all score rows and grouped summaries. These receipts support an actual finite execution record; they do not convert early stopping, low residual, local geometry or offline oracle choices into an identifiability certificate.
