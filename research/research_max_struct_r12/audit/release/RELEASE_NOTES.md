Raw evidence contains 12 verified logical packages (11.416 GB), distributed as 89 ordered transport parts of at most 128 MiB. Download PUBLICATION_PARTS_MANIFEST.json and use restore_release_assets.py with an explicit output directory to join the requested package. Most packages are ZIP64 wrapped in Zstandard; the supplement is plain ZIP. Decode .zst, then extract ZIP. ARTIFACTS.md and UPLOAD_MANIFEST.json specify exact paths, sizes and coverage. Latest repository reports take precedence over older document snapshots contained in raw packages.

No historical R1–R11 evidence was rewritten. This release is a reproducible research record and paper draft, not a claim of journal acceptance.
