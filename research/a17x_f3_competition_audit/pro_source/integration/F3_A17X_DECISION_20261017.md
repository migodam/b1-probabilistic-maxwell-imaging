## Why the two algorithms are not an immediate mathematical merge

1. F3 prior chart g(q) describes globally supported normalized Gaussian fields. Seven latent material dimensions do not imply a rank-seven DeltaX. For DeltaX=diag(delta chi) with many nonzero cells, its exact physical port support can be nearly the whole current dimension. The assumption DeltaX=U D U* may hold only with large U. Truncating Gaussian tails can modify the exact P0 material or require an extra, quantitative full-output truncation bound. Count every port construction and RHS.
2. Even if A17-X's rational dark-source chart is an exact scattering-equivalent continuum, d_i(t)=t a_i/(1+t gamma_i) will generally leave the original fixed-phase/normalized-Gaussian P0 support. Scalar single-source exactness does not establish shared-material multi-source admissibility or isotropic 3D admissibility.
3. A17-X's complex source-coordinate Jacobian and volume determinant apply only to a locally invertible free-complex-material chart with a declared measure; they cannot replace the seven-dimensional nonuniform F3 quotient law.
4. F3 solves candidate discovery in a nontrivial finite-noise region; exact darkness and F(q')=F(q) are stronger and can be locally impossible. An actual noisy posterior is not the indicator deltaPhi<=2, either.
5. F3 shows that full scalar-TM solves are cheap. Paying for low-rank current bases, Schur actions, tail certificates and full fallback may cost more than an optimized native corrector. This is a decisive empirical question, not a proof of acceleration.

## Conditional obstruction: local injectivity of the prior-restricted map

Let f(q)=W ReIm F(g(q)) be C1 on an admissible chart around q0. Suppose A0=Df(q0) has smallest singular value beta>0 in the DECLARED q/material metric and Df is Lipschitz with constant L on the line segment q0+h, with no illegality crossing. Then the elementary integral/Taylor estimate gives

    ||f(q0+h)-f(q0)|| >= beta||h|| - 0.5 L||h||^2.

Consequently, for 0<||h||<2beta/L, no nonzero exact isodata endpoint is possible in this chart. One-sided allowed h at a prior boundary obeys the same inequality. A17-X's exact dark-source family cannot remain inside that local prior chart and still change material at constant data. This is a classical local-immersion consequence, not a new A17 theorem; beta/L here have NOT been rigorously bounded in P0. F3's finite-precision rank-seven at its particular anchors motivates this negative control, but does not prove global uniqueness or exclude far-separated modes. Finite-noise alternatives are fully compatible with local injectivity.
