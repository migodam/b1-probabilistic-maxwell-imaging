# R3 competing mechanisms and verified primary-source boundary

2026-11-03. Evidence audit only; no inversion, numerical identity test, hidden-GT read, historical edit, or publication. Final mathematical validity, route selection, novelty, and scientific interpretation remain with the parent Codex thread.

## Direct assessment

Artificial-loss continuation has an explicit Maxwell optimization precedent. It is a reasonable development hypothesis, but neither artificial loss nor continuation is a new general mechanism. The useful R3 question is whether a tightly bounded, unchanged-data additive-loss path escapes a particular failed material solution better than the same additional ordinary optimization budget. That comparison must be judged at zero artificial loss using the original data and material metrics.

An exact transformation of finite measured data into the response of an altered medium requires an additional information condition. The elementary factor-through-fibers criterion is a qualification tool, not by itself a new theorem deserving a global originality claim. A concrete Maxwell counterexample with lawful materials, independently verified fibers, nontrivial altered-data separation, and honest numerical scope could nevertheless provide useful mechanism evidence.

## Competing hypotheses and discriminating evidence

1. **DPCS locking rather than missing APCS capacity.** R2's mf12 intervention enlarged APCS while retaining fixed data-derived strong coefficients. Atlas10 F-BI-021 already describes releasing strong noisy coefficients. A controlled release isolates a real remaining mechanism; it has weak originality and is not itself a feasibility certificate. Compare the original Maxwell endpoint and shared-material legality, not only the auxiliary loss.
2. **Auxiliary profile bias rather than insufficient inner accuracy.** R2's exact-current profile had only1/3 joint successes versus mature CIE2/3. Variable projection and its residual metric are already covered by R2's primary WRI references; repeating exact elimination is not a new route. A constrained data ball or augmented Lagrangian may alter exploration, but this is established constrained optimization, and the original physical endpoint still matters.
3. **Material coordinate pathology rather than information gain.** Inverse contrast gives an affine diagonal resolvent when the contrast is nonzero, but creates a singular chart at zero. Fractional-linear maps have poles or high-contrast inverse amplification. A bijective smooth coordinate transports stationary points in the interior; it can change finite optimization trajectories through the metric, not add information or eliminate physical ambiguity.
4. **Loss improves optimization through resonance smoothing.** P5 supports this mechanism in Maxwell design. Against unchanged observations, lossy stages are biased auxiliary objectives, with no guarantee the true lossless material solves them. A later zero-loss corrector is necessary but can return to the same basin. A matched extra-iteration control distinguishes path benefit from additional work.
5. **Spatial geometry moves bypass amplitude-only trapping.** P10 establishes the neighboring shape-evolution idea. Resampling plus free material refinement retains a broad final class but may smooth boundaries, clip material, alter volume, or lose support at the box. These are changes to the proposal, not evidence of extra measurements. A common final physical objective and one predetermined transport family are essential for interpretation.
