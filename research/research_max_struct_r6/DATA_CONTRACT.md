# R6 public data and independent scoring contract

`data_public/cases.json` is the only case inventory for inversion. All paths are relative to repository root. It contains 15 distinct previously disclosed material objects (`DEV_ALL_DISCLOSED`) and six new noisy material objects (`NEW_CONFIRMATION_UNSCORED`). Historical objects are the FBG-R3 metadata's 12 clean amplitude-12 cases (one per object), plus the distinct R2 b20d and R4 42cb/63ab cases. Their original observations/acquisitions are retained; historical layouts are not all uniform and clean historical measurements must not be called fresh confirmations.

Load only G/E0/S and permitted geometry from `operators_file`, then Y/noise/prior fields from `observation_file`. Shared operators are referenced in place; no duplicate large operator files are made. The original R2 b20d archive contains both its operators and observation. A legacy operator's `material_bounds` does not override the R6 contract's [.02,20]. Never merge fields from truth archives.

New observations use the unchanged existing disk-pulse scalar-TM VIE `research.research_z_alg_r4_diversity.src.physics.Model.forward`. The shared operator source is `research/research_z_alg_r3_digits/public_data/operators.npz`: 24x24 cells, 576 independent real material unknowns, 12 full-aperture uniform sources, 48 full-aperture receivers, spacing .04, wavelength 1, pulse radius .018, k=2π. Domain width .96 wavelengths. No new physics backend, assembly, font representation, template basis, or reduced material inversion is introduced. Cell material fractions are integrated at 16x16 subcell samples; the complete 576-entry material vector is forwarded on the same grid used for inversion. This is explicitly a same-discretization inverse-crime comparison, not a cross-grid validation.

The six authorized analytic geometry families have independent small rigid/scale perturbations drawn under the fixed private seed, then shuffled and given opaque random IDs. Their private generation records assert material thickness at least two cell widths and independent gaps at least 1.5 cells. Per-ID geometry, family, seed, GT, mask, clean signal and noise are withheld in `sealed_truth/confirmation/`. No family-to-ID mapping, array or image is supplied to inversion or the scientific coordinator before prediction sealing. The generator script is custodian-only and is not an inversion dependency.

New material is .1 + 12*cell_fraction. Each clean observation uses one complete retained LU with all 12 sources as RHS. Independent complex circular normal noise satisfies E|noise_ij|²=σ², σ=.001*||Yclean||/sqrt(576); each real component has variance σ²/2. Public sigma and the original 99%-CN noise radius follow the prior study's convention. Actual realized relative noise need not equal exactly .001. Bounds [.02,20], host start .1, amplitude scale 12, smooth/TV weights .0001, TV epsilon .01, prior width 19.5 are identical across compared methods. These metadata are public prior assumptions, not a restriction to the generation family.

`sealed_truth/scoring/historical_mapping_PRIVATE.json` points to the previously released historical scoring truth and holds its presentation labels. Truth is private to the independent scorer. All algorithms/configurations must be frozen before consuming new observations; all candidate dispositions/predictions must be sealed before scoring. Scores may not select roots or revise predictions. No inversion was run by this generator. No additional scientific gate or hash check was introduced.

Generation costs, timing scope, physical residuals and the one failed pre-forward inventory attempt are in `audit/GENERATION_RECEIPT.json`. Imports/process startup and the receipt's own final write are outside its internal timer; both observed process durations are retained in the audit. Publication is not part of this data task.
