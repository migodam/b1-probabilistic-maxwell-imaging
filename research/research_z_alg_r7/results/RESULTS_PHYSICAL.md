# TM VIE recovery of one prescribed contrast

The target is the center of a known 49-cell host. Nine material coordinates vary independently, and a single receiver records one complex value per shot. Two coherent illuminations synthesize the two incident directions needed for that target. The regular, visible target is uniquely determined from the two clean measurements in the ideal reduced model. Emitter power and source conditioning determine whether the noisy measurements give a useful interval.

## Experimental geometry and data

- Each case uses nonoverlapping circular pulse cells of radius $0.041\lambda$ at $k_0=2\pi/\lambda$. The 7-by-7 lattice has compact pitch $0.145\lambda$ or expanded pitch $0.30\lambda$. The nine active cells occupy every second row and column among the interior five rows and columns. The center is global zero-based cell 24 and zero-based active coordinate 4. The other 40 cells have a spatially varying, exactly known contrast. The explicit function and parameters are in [the experiment](../src/physical_experiment.py).
- The full ring and 150° arc each expose 20 coherent external line sources at radius $1.55\lambda$. The receiver is at $(0.38,1.10)\lambda$. Source geometry, the support, the target, both pitches, and the aperture choices were set before drawing or evaluating unknown materials. The expanded pitch was included after the compact geometry-only spectrum showed a weak mode. The 24 material vectors use independent uniform draws in $[0.5,30]^9$ from seed 9172626 and are paired across layouts.
- The emitted power of a physical feed $a$ is $a^*Qa$, with $Q_{lm}=J_0(k_0|s_l-s_m|)$ from the full angular far-field integral. The setup uses the R2 pulse VIE and exact fixed-host reduction, and the R5 power Gram. All methods share the source modes with eigenvalues at least $1/16$. This is a sufficient condition for a unit-power shot to satisfy $\|a\|_2\leq4$. The two exact target directions are synthesized by their minimum-power preimages within those modes. The two actual feeds comprise a power-orthonormal basis of their preimage span. The experiment keeps the Gram spectrum, incident singular values, projection defects, shot amplitudes and energies.
- Observations come from independently factoring each full 49-by-49 VIE and applying the coherent weights to the complete line-source response. Host response is then subtracted. This is a separate forward solve using the same discretized Green operator as the reduced model. The complex noise vector is drawn uniformly from the ball of radius $\sqrt{s}\,\sigma$ for $s$ measured shots. The known radius is used in inference with a $3\times10^{-12}$ absolute numerical margin. The sensitivity sweep uses $\sigma=0,10^{-6},10^{-4},5\times10^{-4},2\times10^{-3}$ in the TM response normalization.
- Each focused and random shot radiates one unit. Random two-shot feeds use an unselected seeded complex-orthonormal pair in the same retained power modes. The complete power-whitened SVD basis uses nine shots at total energy two, with a separate energy-nine, equal-unit-shot reference. The energy-two comparison also includes favorable completion with total detector radius $\sqrt{2}\,\sigma$, matching the focused pair. In this extra condition the nine-shot noise consequently has a lower per-shot scale.
- The scalar inversion keeps every box value $q$ obeying $|d(c_e-q c_H)-qR_i|\le(\epsilon+3\times10^{-12})\|c_e-q c_H\|_2$, where $d$ is the measured row, $c_e,c_H$ are coefficients in the focused shot basis, and $\epsilon$ is the detector radius. This is a necessary scalar condition for feasibility, so the hull can be used as an outer interval. Its midpoint is the reported point estimate. The same formula applies to a complete source basis using its own synthesis coefficients and detector radius. Point comparisons additionally include the clipped real part of the direct completion quotient and conventional box-constrained TRF with an analytic material Jacobian and 12 initializations. The best residual selects the TRF estimate.
- Target extrema from 12 TRF endpoints and two local constrained refinements are validated with full VIE solves. Two accepted endpoints establish that the feasible scalar range contains those two distinct target values under the declared noise radius. The spread measures a lower bound on the diameter. The focused inequality gives an outer interval, so its width and the demonstrated witness diameter have different meanings.

## Results

All four source libraries have rank nine in the retained power modes. The largest relative defect across the two synthesized directions is $2.06\times10^{-13}$, and the largest measured Euclidean feed norm is 1.43 for a unit-radiated-power focused shot. The independent full/reduced library responses agree to $2.36\times10^{-16}$ absolute across all 96 material/layout states. The median relative Born response error around the host is 1.59–1.72 by layout, indicating that the acquired data include substantial multiple scattering. The maximum clean absolute target error of the two-shot midpoint over all states is $5.86\times10^{-11}$.

The intervals contained all 480 focused truths across materials, layouts and noise levels. Median focused widths at $\sigma=10^{-6}$ were 1.866 and 1.040 in the compact layouts, and 0.0049 and 0.0192 in the expanded layouts. At $\sigma=2\times10^{-3}$, the hull spanned the entire box in 4/24 expanded-ring and 19/24 expanded-arc cases. The scalar inequality sets in those full-width cases can be disconnected. The scalar set itself covered the entire box in 0/24 expanded-ring and 7/24 expanded-arc cases. At $\sigma=10^{-4}$, focused observation norms were 42.8 and 2.4 dB above the detector radius in the median compact ring and arc, and 49.5 and 36.7 dB above it in the expanded cases. High overall response SNR can coexist with poor target conditioning.

At $\sigma=10^{-4}$ the median expanded ring and arc outer widths under energy-two, nine-shot completion were 2.504 and 6.648 with the same detector noise per shot. They remained 1.203 and 3.756 when the total detector radius was matched as well. The equal-energy direct quotient had median absolute errors of 0.304 and 0.707. Using the full real material model in nine-shot TRF gave much lower errors in the compact layouts and comparable errors in the expanded layouts. With nine unit-power shots, TRF median errors were 0.028 and 0.081 in the expanded layouts. The two-shot result establishes selective acquisition and an explicit outer interval. The point-error ordering varies across noise, geometry and estimator.

At $\sigma=10^{-4}$, the median independently verified random two-shot target witness spans were 26.99 and 26.03 for the two expanded layouts. In expanded-ring case 0 the target truth is 3.95117. Its focused interval was $[3.89465,4.03062]$, and full VIE feasible witnesses approached both endpoints. The random pair admitted full VIE witness targets of 1.35047 and 30 with residual norms below $3.08\times10^{-6}$ against a radius of $1.4142\times10^{-4}$. In the compact layouts the median validated spread under the focused data was 29.50 and 29.45, confirming large actual ambiguity in these examples. The recorded witness searches sometimes found no candidate at a very small tolerance. In that case the numeric outer interval and any optimizer endpoints remain separate evidence.

A new geometry required one 49-by-49 host factorization with 29 complex right-hand sides, plus illumination algebra. Host setup took 1.8–2.4 ms CPU and source design took 0.14–0.26 ms CPU in the recorded run. At $\sigma=10^{-4}$ the median scalar interval evaluation took 33–41 μs CPU and used no material solves. The median same-data TRF took 60–133 ms CPU per material and used 430–1,132 reduced factorizations over 12 starts. These run times exclude full VIE acquisition simulation and post-inversion witness checks. Some solver starts reached their evaluation limit. All endpoints remain in the raw traces.

## Reproduction

Run from the repository root with a Python environment containing NumPy, SciPy and Matplotlib. The script imports the shared `research/research_z_alg_r2/src/physics.py` operator. The local sparse checkout also resolves that file through the historical publication worktree.

```bash
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 uv run --with numpy --with scipy --with matplotlib \
  python research/research_z_alg_r7/src/physical_experiment.py
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 uv run --with numpy --with scipy --with matplotlib \
  python research/research_z_alg_r7/src/plot_physical.py
```

The [individual rows](physical_individual_rows.csv) hold errors and intervals by layout, material, noise and arm. The [raw traces](physical_raw.json) include solver endpoints and independently re-evaluated witness vectors. The [observations and feeds](physical_observations.npz) contain the complex arrays and material truth. The [summary](physical_summary.json) and [paper figure](../figures/physical_comparison.pdf) are derived from those records.
