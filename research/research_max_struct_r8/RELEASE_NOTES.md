# MAX-STRUCT-R8 — Neural initialization, current transport and model adequacy

Completed research package: 42 independent synthetic materials, 750 primary/initialization predictions and 126 separately frozen affine-current predictions, all sealed before test-truth scoring. Actual neural training, original-paper Austria adaptations, 25 measured Fresnel endpoints and 52 small vector-Maxwell endpoints are retained. Endpoint counts are not independent statistical samples.

The clearest synthetic benefit is a 24,737-parameter neural initializer followed by direct physical optimization. Simply connecting it to sourcewise/global A+ often loses that benefit. Affine-current repair is partial; choosing the lower final physical objective from two paid branches improves the ID cohort but worsens held-out-family and cross-grid material error. Classical, measured and 3D comparisons do not establish a generally superior A+ solver.
