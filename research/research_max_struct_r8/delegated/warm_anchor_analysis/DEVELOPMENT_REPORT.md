# Fixed physical-complement anchor: DEVELOPMENT

The fixed anchor retains JD+(I-PD)jphys(m0) and optimizes the existing Fourier coefficients. The experiment has no extra optimized degree of freedom; computational projection/storage are charged by solver receipts. Interpretation, scientific claims and novelty judgment remain with the root.

All predefined development/confirmation objects are retained, including failures. Original NN Dual/global A+ have matched unanchored development baselines; sourcewise A+ has NO identical unanchored development baseline. Its comparison to NN direct is descriptive, not a matched anchor ablation. The separate42-object confirmation has all three same-arm anchor/material-NN pairs and the critical global A+ anchor vs nn_joint comparison. nn_joint projects the physical initial current into the old truncated affine family; the anchor retains the full physical APCS offset. Dual/sourcewise lack an identical truncated physical-current projection control, so their anchor comparisons combine current initialization and affine-space changes and cannot isolate a pure high-mode effect. Development observations cannot establish confirmation performance.

Absolute solver/warm/cold CPU are stratified by hardware. feature_cpu_seconds and inference_cpu_seconds are original Mac timings; deployment_prefix_cpu_seconds is the same-solver-hardware independent measurement for confirmation. Validation prefixes reuse actual Mac preprocessing/inference records and are labelled separately. Training remains a separate cost.

Paid original-Phi incumbents determine budget states; material scores never select an iterate. Matched endpoints use per-object min(finalA,finalB), report unused allowance/checkpoint gaps, and are same allowance rather than equal actual spend. At-most-budget curves retain a stopped method’s paid terminal state without continuing computation. Missing/failed checkpoint availability is explicit. Bootstrap material effects resample independent objects; no significance claim. No new inverse solve is performed by this analysis.
