# Warm confirmation analysis

Primary comparisons use 630 = 42 x 15 tasks, with ID/OOD/cross-grid cohorts separate. The additional 120 tasks and their matched controls are reported only on the fixed eight-object subset. Failures remain in denominators; available continuous pairs are explicitly counted.

Cost fields: feature_cpu_seconds and inference_cpu_seconds are the original actual Mac timings. deployment_prefix_cpu_seconds is the independent preprocessing/inference measurement on each solver hardware. Solver CPU, same-machine warm deployment CPU, and actual mixed-machine workflow CPU have separate columns. Absolute CPU comparisons are stratified by hardware.

Training amortization uses the original 1536 training / 192 validation generation outer receipts (including feature/setup work once) plus the full physics 120-epoch training receipt. Born/domain costs enter the broader scientific setup ledger, not this deployment numerator. K uses arithmetic mean independent-object paired uniform-minus-NN warm deployment CPU savings, never a median. Mac results are CPU bookkeeping. XINAN results require K=(cMac*Ttrain)/(cXINAN*mean_saved_CPU); the unspecified price ratio prevents a numeric economic break-even. Nonpositive mean savings give no finite amortization advantage. Costs of failed branches remain reported; saving time does not establish equivalent material quality.

Two-branch portfolios use the already frozen public original-Phi selection and charge both full solvers with a shared NN prefix once. No unfrozen partial-branch budget schedule is invented. The strict all-method common-overlap curves are retained as a diagnostic and can end early. Additional object-paired endpoints use min(final_cost_A, final_cost_B) as the common allowance for each object and hardware, with last-paid original-Phi incumbents. Unused allowance and checkpoint bracketing gaps are explicit: equal at-most allowance does not imply equal actual spend. Primary budget curves use sparse paid original-Phi incumbents, not GT-picked iterations. Object-paired bootstrap intervals are descriptive and carry no significance declaration.
