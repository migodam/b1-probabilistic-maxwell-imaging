# Research G: closest-literature matrix

Review date: 2026-10-15. This is a targeted primary-source review, not a claim of exhaustive literature coverage. F = accessible main-text/method review; A = abstract/publisher-record review only; O = official implementation/project documentation. PDF text could be retrieved for some older papers, but the browser PDF rendering service failed; no uninspected figure is used as scientific evidence here. Publication status is reported conservatively when only a preprint was verified.

## A. Inverse problems, topology and generative reconstruction

## What is NOT a supported novelty claim

Neither multiple generated images, a GAN/diffusion proposal followed by a forward check, a topology derivative followed by GN, a learned material/current representation, an unstructured QD archive, nor group-aware RL is independently new.

The unresolved target for Research G is the JOINT system: nonlinear Maxwell-compatible material worlds, retained structural differences after correction, no prescribed topology taxonomy, finite-K coverage gains, matched total physics cost, and cross-object evidence. The targeted search did not establish an existing paper meeting all those requirements, but that is not proof of first priority.
