# What finite multistatic Maxwell data retain after quadratic elimination

### Abstract

For a finite reciprocal scalar scattering model we ask which hidden currents can be determined after a fixed projection cancels the quadratic constitutive residual. We prove a generic rank formula for its fixed quadratic coefficient and give the surviving linear spectrum when the constant vector is its only left null direction. All computed 576- and 1024-cell operators exhibited this case. With 48 receivers and 12 illuminations, at most 66 of 6,336 complex dark-current coordinates survived. Turning to differences between physical materials, we derive a localized exterior-product identity whose two-cell interaction coefficient also occurs in a three-atom multistatic port space. Distances to these spaces rule out arbitrary two-cell repairs at eight previously recorded reconstruction endpoints. In two cases even an unrestricted rank-two port bound was inconclusive. A finite-precision bound using the physical port spaces excluded repairs at 1.186 and 1.271 times the archived discrepancy radius. Sparse changes could be reconstructed when a reference medium and accurate difference data were available. Equal-cost full-image experiments and held-out experimental measurements did not show an improvement over established material reconstruction endpoints.

### Introduction and related work

Subspace optimization and contraction integral equations simplify scattering inversion by treating measured and unmeasured current components differently. Multi-illumination effective-source reconstruction, T-matrix completion, and exact support-restricted multiple scattering have separate precedents. The questions here concern the information that survives its convexification and the converse information retained by physically supported changes. ### Model and results

Let $E=B+GJ$, $J=XE$, with real diagonal $X$ and reciprocal $G^T=G$. Let $N$ be an orthonormal basis of the receiver null space of dimension $d$. The fixed curvature rows are $N_i\wedge(GN)_i$. The edge joining two cells contributes an exterior product with opposite signs in the two rows. A labelled tree realizes $\min\{n-1,\binom d2\}$ independent contributions, yielding the generic rank in the class of unrestricted reciprocal finite matrices. For the constant-only left null space the surviving equation is the skew part of $J^TB$. Its nontrivial row singular values are $\sqrt{(\lambda_a^2+\lambda_b^2)/n}$ with $\lambda$ the padded singular values of $N^TB$. A full-rank claim for the source-pair rows would still leave an extensive hidden-current kernel. [Proofs and degeneracies](THEORY.md)

For a legal finite change supported on $I$, write $U_I$ for the reference total fields, $O_I$ for the dressed receiver columns and $T$ for the exact local response matrix. The port change is $O_I T U_I$. The spatial row of the secant exterior product vanishes off $I$ and equals $\sum_j T_{ij}u_i\wedge u_j$ on $I$. On two cells the off-diagonal coefficient multiplies both the internal alternating term and the external pattern $o_i u_j+o_j u_i$. Its component orthogonal to the two single-cell patterns determines the conditional worst-case noise sensitivity. Profiling over all three complex port coefficients relaxes the physical two-cell model and produces a computable lower bound. All location pairs must be included when using this bound for exclusion. ### Experiments

Twenty-one archived measurements from ten distinct simulated materials supplied the full-rank tests. A five-disk physical material objective had an interval-certified strict interior nonglobal minimum at 32.42% material error and 0.6764% port residual. For the whole-image problem, we compared local finite actions, Born and random joint actions, an ordinary restart and physical continuation on common endpoints. Under the same additional two CPU seconds and across measured wall-clock prefixes there were no new whole-image recoveries. Three classic Austria conditions and the experimental FoamTwinDiel target supplied an external check. From the mature Dual-CIE endpoint the held-out complex-field error of the finite two-cell step was 0.5012, compared with 0.4573 after four CPU seconds of ordinary continuation. For known-background monitoring, 24 parent media each supplied two planted changes. A four-pair shortlist with direct conditional reads recovered 47/48 supports under noise equal to 10% of the change signal. The same shortlist size without the cross atom achieved the same count. On independent fine/coarse grids the full-ring and one-sided line-array counts were 11/12 and 5/12. In matched three-dimensional double-polarization Maxwell simulations, the hemisphere recovered 4/4, and the planar dictionary recovered 0/4 at 10% differential noise. A two-sided subspace search recovered the clean planar cases, while noisy planar high-order modes lay below the noise scale. ### Discussion and limitations

The finite rank theorem does not establish genericity within every physically parameterized Green family or convergence as the mesh is refined. Our exact exclusion concerns the archived discrete model, its specified discrepancy radius and a given starting medium. In 11/12 original SRAP conditions the clean fine/coarse mismatch alone exceeded the stated noise level. Two-cell support and an accurate reference medium are strong additional assumptions for change monitoring. A bound on the number of changed cells is compatible with a single collective material parameter that moves many cells. We did not establish that a small set of such coordinates can be inferred from unknown-target measurements. [Model error](MODEL_GAP.md) · [Negative results](NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md)

### Conclusion

The most defensible result is a distinction between admissible algebraic reduction and observable material change. Exact annihilation can leave little measurable information. Physical port spans can nevertheless exclude whole families of finite repairs, even when residual rank admits them. Constructing accessible, useful regional changes under model error remains open.
