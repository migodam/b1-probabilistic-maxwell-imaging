## Algebra and measure check

- **KL direction is correct.** With P=pY*pi_G and Q=pY*Q_G, the chain rule gives KL(P||Q)=E_Y KL(pi_G||Q_G). Mapping (Y,C) to the event indicator yields BernKL(a||q), not BernKL(q||a). Its derivative in q is (q-a)/(q(1-q)); hence substituting a lower confidence limit L>a is conservative. Infinite KL when absolute continuity fails causes no contradiction.
- **The conditional/joint TV equality is correct** for standard Borel observation/material spaces, measurable probability kernels, and the convention TV(P,Q)=sup_A |P(A)-Q(A)|=half the L1 measure distance. The equality uses their identical Y marginal. Both the equality and the event lower bound can fail in this form if the model's Y marginal differs. No pointwise-in-Y lower bound follows from the average.
- **Material atoms and degenerate support are harmless.** A noninjective decoder, saturated plateau atoms, and low-dimensional material support do not require a pixel Lebesgue density. The argument applies directly to pushforward probability measures. The essential condition is that E is measurable from (Y,C) itself: Phi must be the original full material-based likelihood. A statistic depending on latent coordinates lost by the decoder would not automatically give a material-law lower bound.
- **P(E)=.025 is unconditional under the original joint generative law.** A fresh exact posterior draw coupled to Y has that same joint law, so its residual has the original noise distribution after averaging over Y. This is not the claim that the PIT is uniform for each fixed Y. The Gamma256 assertion depends on the exact discrete forward model, all 256 independent proper complex noise entries, their specified variance convention/whitening, and native original observations. The compact summary records the intended shape/scale but does not independently prove the physical/noise contract. The proof already states this limitation.

## Finite-sample confidence and adversarial caveats

1. **CP + Bonferroni is mathematically appropriate under the stated ideal IID-parent experiment.** For each fixed model/cohort, index0 must be one independent draw from Q_G(.|Y_i), with IID Y_i from the same pY. Then indicators are IID Bernoulli(q), even though their success probabilities conditional on the observed Y_i vary. Across-model and across-cohort dependence does not invalidate Bonferroni. Therefore common random numbers between models are allowed; independent training seeds are not needed for this eight-fixed-model claim.
2. **The actual independent-stream qualification must remain visible.** The evaluator derives a distinct per-parent PRNG seed with SeedSequence([sample_seed,index]), then fixes index0 before truth access. This supports the intended implementation; it is not a mathematical proof that deterministic pseudorandom streams are independent uniforms. The exact CP statement is over ideal sampling of both parents and model randomization. Conditional on a single frozen random tape, indicators can be independent but nonidentically distributed, so the usual binomial CP guarantee does not automatically follow. Reusing one identical latent draw across all parents would give a sharper counterexample: common randomization can induce dependence or a conditional law different from the intended Q. No such shared-parent latent seed appears in the checked snippet. Finite PRNG seed collisions/finite precision are also outside exact certification.
3. **Pre-frozen panel versus post-hoc interpretation is separated clearly in the prose.** The eight model/cohort pairs, threshold .975, and index0 are asserted to be the old fixed PPC panel; the lower-bound transformation itself is explicitly POST_HOC_INTERPRETATION. If those eight pairs or the threshold were selected after seeing these counts from a larger search, eight-way Bonferroni would be insufficient. The compact evidence supports the exact eight names/counts and fixed index0, but by itself does not establish pre-observation chronology or prove threshold pre-registration. Parent should cite the existing frozen PPC manifest/chronology when publishing, rather than imply this review independently authenticated them. No additional data reads are needed to state this limitation.
4. **Numerical classification error is an assumption, not certified away.** The score summary has all planned parents finite and zero failures for all eight comparisons, so there is no observed finite-only deletion in these counts. But neither numerical forward error nor PIT-threshold crossing error is bounded by this compact summary. Gamma CDF rounding to 1 for huge residuals is not itself harmful to the >.975 indicator; errors near the threshold could matter. The proof's non-certification caveat is necessary. If only P(E) in [a-epsilon,a+epsilon] were defensible, the TV lower bound would become max(0,L-a-epsilon), and for L>a+epsilon the conservative forward-KL bound is BernKL(a+epsilon||L). Unbounded physical/numerical mismatch defeats the numerical bound as stated.

## Finite-NFE CFM law

The sample-law bound does not require a CFM log density, invertibility of a discrete integration map, a successful divergence integral, or qualification of the continuous-ODE density. It applies to the **actual fixed NFE32 sampling algorithm's material pushforward law**, under the same ideal randomization convention as the other samplers. The proof correctly says a qualified log-density is unnecessary; adding the explicit words “fixed NFE32 sampling law” would remove ambiguity.
