Checked material_moments.py, prepare.py, validate_moments.py, train_matched.py, rrr_controls.py, original R4 density/fusion implementations, and current per-run receipts. Targeted checks are in B_REVIEW_TARGETED_QA.json.

  • PASS: original-frame covariance is F.T @ diag(exp(2 logscale)*x_scale**2) @ F, matching original density sampling. The implementation retains field covariance and checks field/property cross covariance before using the independent formula. It does not silently replace a correlated field/property law with a product law.
  • PASS with estimand qualification: channels are meanRe, meanIm, marginal varianceRe, varianceIm of the original decoder. The deterministic population variance is the high-K limit. A K32 empirical ddof0 variance has expectation31/32 of population variance; these channels have different finite-K expectations. Frozen-head substitution is a distribution-shift diagnostic, while matched-head training is the relevant comparison. Do not describe the two finite-K feature laws as identical.
  • PASS: source covariance whitening followed by output/input crosscovariance SVD learns predictable residual directions, rather than PCA of material GT. Ranks2/4/8/16 and ridge settings are chosen using VAL only. Targeted header/string read verified TRAIN1024 and VAL256 parent IDs are disjoint.
  • REPORTING LIMIT: fivefold OOF refits the correction coefficients only. Input standardization/PCA are fit on all TRAIN inputs, and the frozen baseline was trained on the same historical TRAIN parents. This is conditional correction OOF, not end-to-end cross-fitting or independent risk estimation. The current receipt partly discloses the baseline scope; the PCA scope also needs explicit wording.
  • COST GAP: head-only parameter counts and provider-only deterministic timing cannot establish the compression gate. Full warm prediction must include frozen provider encoder, covariance/phase moments, feature normalization, risk head and any baseline/feature projection/regression used by RRR.   • FAILURE/RESOURCE GAP: matched training raises on nonfinite loss or3GiB RSS but has no top-level failure receipt/finally block. RSS checks occur only every100updates and after tensor allocation. Preserve failed attempt metadata and logs, guard initial CPU/device allocations, and report MPS allocation separately from host RSS. Existing initial RSS and recurrence failures are preserved, which is correct.
  • SEED SEMANTICS: raw-y, decoder and physical RRR fits are deterministic conditional on a paired frozen baseline and fold assignment; three old baseline seeds give three distinct residual problems. They are not three independent stochastic correction optimizations. Random-spectrum projection and OOF partitions are seeded stochastic components. Count four RRR representation arms, each paired with three baseline seeds, rather than inventing twelve representation arms.
  • NUMERICS: the stable transition integration and explicit zero-field branch are appropriate. The recurrence fallback is guarded and tested, but remains numerical; arbitrary future covariance/provider changes must pass the existing field/property coupling guard and fresh quadrature checks. Variance clipping at zero is rounding handling, not evidence of posterior calibration.
