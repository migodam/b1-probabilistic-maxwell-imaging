# Core48 visual observations

This is a post-seal descriptive intake of all 48 core objects (`r13_fresh_0000`–`r13_fresh_0047`), based on actual inspection of all 24 saved diagnostic PNG pages. It records visible geometry, contrast distribution, and artifacts; it assigns no success/failure bins, score cutoff, new success rate, or aggregate method ranking. The parent retains final qualitative categories and scientific interpretation.

The displayed scale is derived from the truth maximum and shared by every method within each row; each panel labels saturation counts. Yellow therefore may hide amplitude differences above that row maximum. A dark depression is described as a cavity only when that is the reference geometry; smooth low material in a filled body is distinguished from an empty hole. Blur makes small holes, weak objects, and thin bridges uncertain. The separate absolute [0,20] gallery exists but was not part of this intake. No scoring CSV, GT NPZ, model solve, forward action, optimization, or candidate selection was used or changed. `random_integer` was visible in group 2 but is outside the requested per-method notes.

Both full-capacity controls have separate columns; they are not collapsed into a single outcome. CPU and elapsed review time were not measured and are unknown (null in the receipt).
