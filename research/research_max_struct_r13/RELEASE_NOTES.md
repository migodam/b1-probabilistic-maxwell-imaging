# MAX-STRUCT R13 — completed research evidence

48 fresh independent 2D materials; finite material events, full-state reflection memory, G19/current representation, neural starts and corrector controls; genuine vector-DDA inversion, Austria adaptation and measured Fresnel holdout prediction.

962 canonical method runs. Additional recovery aliases and failed attempts are retained without increasing the independent material count. All new predictions and public selection indices were sealed before joint truth authorization.

Main result: DR and G19 have complementary conditional recovery benefits. The analytic normal-ray event law is valid under its assumptions, but event thresholds did not demonstrate a general unique recovery advantage over simple controls. A+ subset positives and all negative examples are retained. Cross-grid/Mie/calibration limitations qualify the physics claims.
