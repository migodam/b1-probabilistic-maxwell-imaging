## Mechanism and selection audit

- The successful fixed beta arm **does not satisfy the proposed tangent-attenuation explanation** at the hard 12/24 anchors: `||(G+B)T||²/||GT||²` is `2.87/3.09` for high digit 8 and `2.76/3.16` for high curved bar. The selected pilot instead chose `diag/zero/self` on digit 8 and `shuffle/self/self` on curved bar across 8/12/24 stages. Its one-step physical pilot misranked the beta arm for long-run success on these cases. Do not claim that reducing this fitted tangent energy caused the observed improvements.
- A separate **parent-derived local identity**, independently checked algebraically in this review, offers a precise alternative mechanism to test. At uniform public anchor `m=b1`, real scalar `β>0`, `a=1+βb`, reference scale `sβ=a²`, fixed target, and full-column-rank `Q`, let `H=Q*Q`, `W=RH⁻¹`, `D=W*W`, `d_i=(QDQ*)_{ii}≥0`, and `f₀=(c/2)tr(RH⁻¹R*)`. Then the normalized feedback proposal has the **same value** as `f₀` at the anchor but `∇f̂β−∇f₀=−cβ/a·d` for real material coordinates. The identity follows because `(G*+βI)/a−G*=βL*/a` with `L*=I−bG*`; the shared regularizer cancels. This is a local contrast-raising gradient bias. It does not imply that increasing contrast matches truth, that the bias persists away from the uniform anchor, or that the basin expands. Its empirical explanatory value requires a prospectively fixed public-data rule and a later held-out test. A threshold `t_i=g₀,i/(c d_i)` gives a strict sign flip of a positive gradient only when `d_i>0`, `0<t_i<1/b`, and `β>t_i/(1−bt_i)`; equality gives zero, not a strict flip.
- Further exact corollaries identify what activates this local bias. With `Q†=H⁻¹Q*`, the matrix `C_R=R Q†=WQ*` has column energies `d_i=||C_R[:,i]||²`; therefore `Σ_i d_i=||C_R||_F²=ψ₀`. Under invertible source mixing `Q→QC`, `R→RC`, the identity `(QC)†=C⁻¹Q†` makes each `d_i` basis-invariant on the full-rank domain. For any data-extension matrix `W₀` satisfying `W₀E=Y`, use `j=XQ`, `E=(I−GX)Q`, and `C_Y=SX−W₀(I−GX)` to obtain `R=C_YQ` and `C_R=C_Y P_Q`, `P_Q=QQ†`. The extension is nonunique, but its projection onto the observed field span is fixed. The uniform-material directional gradient difference is exactly `−cβ/a·ψ₀`. If the observed data are `Y=F_ref+εZ` at this anchor, with nonzero `F_ref` and fixed `Z`, then `R=−εZ` and the shift is `O(ε²)` as `ε→0`; the normalization `c` stays bounded. At a uniform exact-data root, the normalized beta and zero-feedback **data-term Hessians** coincide: `R=0` removes residual-dependent second-derivative terms and `sβ=a²` cancels `Hβ=a²H`. The common regularizer has the same Hessian, though a true data root need not be stationary for the regularized total objective. For a coarse nonnegative interpolation matrix `P`, the coordinate shift `−cβ/a Pᵀd` is componentwise nonpositive; a signed latent/decoder Jacobian need not preserve that sign. These statements concern the local finite-dimensional proposal, not global attraction, material truth, or an A17 Schur effect.
- The subsequently computed public-data-only `results/contrast_bias_geometry.json` is consistent with this *local* bias: among coordinates with positive zero-feedback gradient, `β=.2` flips `58.3%` for high digit 8, `55.7%` for high curved bar, and `16.0%` for high ring. Low curved bar and moderate ring had no positive-gradient coordinates at this reference. These are retrospective Stage 1 diagnostics from the same disclosed cases, not independent validation or proof of why long-run recovery occurred.
- The fitted diagonal and shuffled candidates were rejected to zero by the whole-box safety rule in **8 of 15** stage/case records each. All 15 block records were scaled below `0.1`. Their weak observed effect may therefore reflect the admissibility cap and fallback, not a clean test of unconstrained fitted feedback. The exact interval/spectral bound is nevertheless a valuable safety result. Report the realized operator, not just its nominal candidate name.
- All fixed-`B` proposal gradients include the transformed Gram derivative, and Stage 0 gives a one-instance directional check. The physical current/data equality is algebraic. Lower auxiliary `ψ_B`, or a lower source-span/projector derivative, does not prove better physical reconstruction. The executed code uses exact full current tangents. It does not use A17's retained-core Galerkin Schur exchange; any improvement cannot be attributed to that mechanism.
- The scalar-beta gauge is algebraically equivalent, under matching current/contrast conventions, to CIE-I's modified-contrast equation in [Zhong & Xu 2019, Eq. (3)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8320915/). This study tests a **different optimizer**: full physical current retained exactly, a transformed-field source-Gram proposal, then physical correction/ranking. Published CIE-I combines modified contrast with joint contrast-source updates and subspace regularization. The present five-case outcome neither verifies nor falsifies that method. See `audit/PRIOR_WORK_LEDGER.md`.
