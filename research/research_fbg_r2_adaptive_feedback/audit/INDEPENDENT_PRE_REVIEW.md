## Algebra and mechanism

- The fixed-`B` derivation is internally consistent: physical current `j` and residual `R` are unchanged by the gauge, while `V=Q+Bj` satisfies `dV=(G+B)T` for exact physical current tangent `T`. `feedback_value_gradient` includes the Gram derivative through `-(G+B)^* V W^*W`, and the Stage 0 directional error is `1.9e-10` relative on one small complex instance. This is a proposal-objective gradient, not a physical-likelihood gradient. See `theory/STAGE0_THEORY_ATTACK.md` §§1–3, `audit/STAGE0_MATH.json`, and `src/feedback_fit.py:443–469`.
- The fitted operator minimizes **source-whitened transformed-field tangent energy at an anchor**. It does not optimize the field-span projector curvature or prove a larger basin. The production solver uses exact full current tangents (`src/adaptive_solver.py:85–90`); no A17 Galerkin bank or Schur exchange is integrated. Do not attribute any observed gain to A17's Schur mechanism. See `src/feedback_fit.py:307–367` and the Stage 0 attack §6.
- `box_certify` gives an exact diagonal interval bound or a conservative `||B diag(m)||<1` bound across the declared material box (`src/feedback_fit.py:184–243`). This addresses transformation poles, but not loss of source Gram conditioning along a trajectory; `cholesky_ex` only detects non-positive-definiteness, not a large condition number. Report the whole-box scaling/fallback and trajectory Gram diagnostics if a conditioning claim is desired.
- Raw `psi_B` and the transformed source-span projector are invariant to invertible mixing of the same source basis. The production normalization and unwhitened physical loss need not be invariant under nonunitary mixing; a noise covariance must be transported. Stage 0 explicitly exposes this caveat. Stage 3's changing `p` changes physical acquisition; its `per_source` arm grows the sum of source amplitude squares, while `fixed_total` holds that coefficient sum at the 12-source reference. The latter does not automatically equalize in-domain incident-field norm or received SNR across distinct geometries. See `BLIND_PROTOCOL.md` §§Source mixing and Frozen finite stages.

## Actionable audit findings before interpreting Stage 1

1. **Distinguish the executed manifest from the earlier placeholder plan.** The authoritative `data_contract/stage1_remote.json` dispatches 64 development tasks with a 45 s wall limit; it does not declare LU/RHS caps. The earlier `data_contract/stage1_plan.json` has ten placeholder pairs and nominal 2,000 LU / 60,000 RHS allowances, but was not executed and is `RETIRED_NOT_EXECUTED` according to the parent. `adaptive_solver.solve` and resident `executor.solve_task` guard wall time only. Do not count placeholder arms as failures or retroactively apply their caps to Stage 1; still report measured LU/RHS for every executed task and avoid a claim of matched LU/RHS. (`adaptive_solver.py:143–197`, `executor.py:69–86`.)
2. **The mechanism is stagewise refitting, not a residual trigger.** The retired placeholder plan named `feedback_trigger`, `feedback_update_count`, and `accepted_update_count`; the executed runner refits once at each reached resolution stage, freezes `B` during its proposal phase, and records `feedback_records`, but has no residual-dependent trigger or explicit accepted-update field. Describe the implementation literally and mark such trigger evidence `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; do not infer accepted updates from the count of stages. (`stage1_plan.json`, `adaptive_solver.py:143–186`.)
3. **Charge pilot and fit work to the adaptive arm.** `feedback_selected` tests six candidates by one bounded auxiliary-gradient step each and selects using the original physical score, which is a legitimate public-data development pilot. Tangent solves, candidate forwards, gradients, and setup time are real costs. `make_feedback` and `select_feedback` update the lane counter and run inside the task wall clock; verify those costs survive phase-counter subtraction and are included in aggregate comparisons. Selection on physical score avoids choosing the smallest `psi_B`, but it is not an independent test of the selected operator. (`adaptive_solver.py:79–140, 143–197`.)
4. **A failed task currently loses partial cost accounting.** The outer exception receipt writes `partial_costs: NOT_RECOVERED_EXCEPTION`; a failing feedback Gram or other midrun error could therefore hide consumed LU/RHS. Preserve `FAIL` and report its cost as unknown rather than zero; do not exclude failures from denominators. (`adaptive_solver.py:221–242`.)
5. **Baseline comparison requires matching algorithm and total resources.** `native_trf` uses full receiver Jacobians, while feedback arms use L-BFGS proposal plus physical corrector; `gd_all`, `gd_late`, `fixedgram`, and `data_lbfgs` are closer optimizer controls. Compare physical endpoint quality at equal declared wall and report actual work separately; do not call LU/RHS matched. The executed 64-task Stage 1 remote manifest is disclosed development, and its extra arms cannot be treated as independent blind replicates. The retired ten-arm placeholder plan contributes no observed endpoint. (`data_contract/stage1_remote.json`, `src/adaptive_solver.py:157–186`.)
6. **A small transformed-field energy ratio alone cannot establish the mechanism.** `B=-G` would annihilate `dV` while leaving the physical inverse problem unchanged, and unrestricted positive scaling can shrink raw `psi_B` with fixed `R`. For each candidate report the realized box scale, energy ratio, pilot physical score, full physical residual, image metric, and cost. Treat `self`, scalar beta, fitted diagonal/block, and shuffled controls as distinct methods. (`STAGE0_THEORY_ATTACK.md` §§2,5; `adaptive_solver.py:106–140`.)
