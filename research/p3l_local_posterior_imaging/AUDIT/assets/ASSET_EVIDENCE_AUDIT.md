## Exact model contract to preserve

q=(z64,u2), z~N(0,I), u independent U[0,1]^2, reference dz du. log p0=-||z||²/2-32log(2pi), legal box on u. No material Gram multiplier, peak normalization, implicit noise-ball posterior support, gain profiling, or per-parent noise renormalization (formal report 74–98; C appendix 27–51).

Field generator is fixed by configs/ONLINE_TARGET.json and src/p1_material/prior.py: mean 0, DC SD .65, field SD 1, 8x8 DCT excluding duplicate DC, ell=.08, halfwidth .5, known ROI r≤.38 (256 of 576 cells). Inside ROI phi=saturation(f/.5); expanded transition polynomial in f is .5+15f/8-5f³+6f⁵, with plateaus at ±.5. chi=.1+.04i + phi*(A+iB); A=.8+u1, B=.03+.15u2. Outside ROI phi=0. MaterialPrior.A is the field map, not a likelihood Jacobian; names must be disambiguated.

TM24 scalar VIE, wavenumber 2pi, 8 sources, 32 complex receivers per source, total scattered field S j, gain=1. Native array is (32,8); pack concatenates Re32 then Im32 separately for each source. sigma=.00119211798514519 fixed. Complex CN variance sigma²; each real component variance sigma²/2. Phi=||prediction-y||complex²/sigma² = .5||sqrt(2)/sigma * pack(prediction-y)||². Likelihood normalization is -256log(pi sigma²)-Phi. A further .5 on complex residual is a temperature error. Prior material mean is exactly ROI .75+.0925i, host elsewhere (report 54–70).

Only split the 64 Gaussian coordinates in first implementation; d weak means d+2 outer coordinates because properties remain explicit, with 64-d strong Gaussian coordinates. If using anchor-relative coordinates the Gaussian prior is shifted; do not reset both blocks to independent zero mean. FULL, LOCAL_C, and SLICE/PROFILE are different targets (report 100–124, 264–336, 815–845). A 2D weak slice is not a 66D marginal. Gaussian-reference analytic saturation moments are exact under that reference only, not exact under the real Maxwell conditional posterior (report 363–427). Conditional mean is needed in addition to weak mass; covariance requires within-conditional variance too.

## Missing information, protocol tensions and execution blockers

1. No qualified original P1 posterior reference, escape mass, teacher, material-risk gain or working online likelihood interval is present. Candidate banks and 16x4 short P2 chains do not fill these gaps (report 50, 1050–1056; claim C13/C14).
2. LOCAL_C metric/radii/stability and deterministic fallback/caps remain unresolved. Master seed, parent manifest, calibration tolerances, reference target difference/MCSE scale, gradient/HVP costs and method budget allocation are null or unspecified. These are actual unfreezes, not reasons to silently fill defaults.
3. Reference schedule arithmetic needs explicit reconciliation: 4*(1024+4096)=20,480 transitions. At one 8-RHS fresh forward each, one mechanism costs at least 163,840 RHS before setup, gradients or repeated slice proposals. Two such independently scheduled mechanisms cost 327,680 RHS, already above the proposed 300,000 per-deep-reference cap. The protocol can fit a shorter cross-check mechanism but must preregister that allocation and cannot call both full schedules completed. ESS 1000 is an acceptance goal, not allocated states.
4. Stage-II 40,000 RHS per parent is across all posterior methods, excluding anchor; a forward-only budget is at most 5,000 fresh proposals split over the panel. This cannot automatically support the deep-reference schedule for each listed method. Stage-V same issue; report already says reduce method count instead of relabeling short chains as teachers.
5. Main report mandates explicit Gaussian64-only first split, while appendix algorithm B 9.1 illustrates a generic 66-dimensional T mixing properties and uses u for strong residual. The appendix is mathematically general, but copying it literally violates the first implementation convention and makes uniform boundaries curved. Use distinct symbols, Gaussian64 orthogonal T, explicit u_prop2 (report 132,815; appendix 604–656).
6. Appendix algorithm A says 'at budget boundary call Full fallback' (581), whereas first-limit-stop and no overrun are binding. A new implementation must reserve fallback RHS before making a proposal; at hard cap do not pay an unallocated Full solve or report an unresolved decision as exact.
7. An 'exact invariant kernel' does not certify mixing. Pseudo-marginal normalizer must be nonnegative unbiased and current random variables retained on rejection; optional material CV ratio needs strict positive normalizer or the extra zero-event proof. LOCAL_C truncation can violate strict positivity (report 408–427).
8. The machine protocol omits several complete generator fields (DCT normalization/DC SD/ell etc.) that exist in the old config. Freeze a complete copied model config plus geometry, not only the abbreviated model_id. This is missing serialization, not evidence the numerical model changed.
9. All package physics authorization fields are false/proposed. Parent must interpret current user authorization separately; do not alter old authorization or transfer historical unspent balances. New experiment needs its own directory/book.

## Computational and storage constraints

A single complex128 576x576 LU is 5,308,416 bytes before compression; a pair of current/field matrices adds 147,456 bytes. Persisting every LU for 20,480 reference transitions is ~108.7 GB per mechanism/parent just for LU. Sixteen parents are ~1.74 TB; two mechanisms double this. The actual sibling P2 bank is already 6.81 GB. Use bounded in-memory current-state/proposal factors, checkpoint factors selectively, preserve every q/logtarget/decision/cost, and stream material moments. Never silently throw away required raw evidence, but do not make every accepted/rejected latent state carry a unique dense LU archive.

4-chain production material complex samples: 16,384*576*16=150,994,944 bytes (~151 MB) per parent; 16 deep parents ~2.42 GB before diagnostics/other arrays. A full 576x576 complex covariance is ~5.31 MB per parent; per-pixel variance sufficient for MSE trace but not full spatial covariance. All figures/uncertainty summaries must declare whether only diagonal variance was computed (report 404).

32 receiver-adjoint RHS per full-J refresh is supported by the actual P1 implementation and saved factors, in addition to 8 forward RHS at a fresh material, but must still be verified with the new book/backend semantics. No physical solve performed here. Gradient/HVP cannot be priced from this count without actual implementation; do not assume 32 or zero. Dense LU baseline can be faster than many reduced Python callbacks, as P2 showed. 64-core48h in the package is a proposed remote upper bound, not this Apple-Silicon machine's observed runtime. Existing reports' short TM24 physics spans do not predict mixing cost.

## Recommended minimal frozen panel for parent decision

First close a zero-new-physics historical regression panel from P1 A/B and all 24 P2 anchors, including failed anchors, with sanitized observations and paid same-q J recomposition. Freeze inputs by explicit path/key/shape/model/noise, not GT quality. This validates packing, prior, moments and derivative algebra; it yields no fresh scientific risk claim.

For a new bounded decisive panel, recommend 8 independent prior parents chosen by preregistered IDs before truth/anchor generation, each one fixed-noise observation; retain every anchor failure with a single frozen deterministic fallback. Treat this as development/pilot, not a powered population-risk result or replacement of proposed 32/16 campaign. Use FULL joint blocked sampler as reference target; freeze Gaussian weak d=4 and keep both properties explicit. A compact four-column mechanism comparison is: optimized deterministic baseline; fixed strong slice; boundary-aware profile+volume+Gaussian material moments (APPROXIMATE); full joint numerical reference. Add corrected collapsed method only if strong weights are nondegenerate; otherwise record NO-GO. Do not expand d or add networks to rescue the panel.

Choose 2 preregistered deep-reference IDs from these 8, independent of observed GT, plus retain any failed-anchor object in the end-to-end table. Freeze each reference's exact transition allocation, cross-mechanism split, MCSE effect scale, total RHS/wall/storage/fallback caps, and stopping labels before physics. More parents or deeper chains require an explicit revised budget. Report paired per-parent MSE/MAE and headroom with MCSE; a small pilot can expose mechanisms/failure but cannot establish the package's 5% locked-test claim. Select boundary/saturation strata for diagnostics using y/anchor features only; never select parents because GT is close to the weak surface.

For certificate exploration, begin with a small offline replay of paid P2 proposal pairs and frozen uniforms to test code paths, mark bounds empirical/heuristic unless truly enclosing, and compare common-cache Full/Krylov/goal-ROM/A17. Do not use E's reference_error in an online controller. A same-uniform exact replay requires a specified numerical Full tolerance and finite-precision enclosure story (appendix 266–321), not merely 0 observed mismatches.

Parent owns final panel, budgets, novelty, theorem validity and scientific decision. This recommendation authorizes no physics and consumes no historical reserve.
