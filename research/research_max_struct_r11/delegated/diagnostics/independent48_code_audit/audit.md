## Nonblocking semantics/coverage observations

**P3 — Paid-state salvage from a failed child needs an explicit reporting interpretation, not an implicit success claim.** `twod_independent_score.py:88–98,162–167` permits a terminalFAILED child with a sealed eligible incumbent to receive its material metric/pass, while retaining task_status. `twod_actual_cpu_fairness_seal.py:19–26` similarly selects a valid paid trace without checking FINISHED. In contrast `twod_secondary_seal.py:64` explicitly requires FINISHED and therefore will not deploy a numerically failed trial. This is not demonstrated data corruption: known snapshots have zero science failures and the primary material gate can reasonably be separate from execution status. The final report should keep these two notions distinct if a future failure occurs; do not silently call a failed attempt a successful algorithm execution. No protocol change is proposed by this audit.

**P3 — Future scoring/render CPU is outside the current48 compiler scope.** `twod_independent_costs.py:28–35` incorporates the three public seal children/controllers but has no future main scorer, secondary reindex or plot producer cost. Those operations had not run at review time, so this is a future coverage gap, not a claim of currently duplicated/missing completed science. The main scorer's absolute self timer (`twod_independent_score.py:212`) includes imports through receipt preparation but not its final write/exit; a later external child receipt should replace, not be added to, that self timer. Final all-study bookkeeping must explicitly include those disjoint postprocess processes after execution and cease reporting GT_scoring='NOT_RUN' as an unconditional field. Existing completed inversion CPU comparison is unaffected.
