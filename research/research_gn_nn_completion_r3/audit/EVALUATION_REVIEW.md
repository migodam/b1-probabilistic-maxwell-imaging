# Evaluation and offline scoring review

Implementation scope: truth-free finite candidate generation/verification/correction/selection, separate prediction closure and truth scoring, and all-case figures. This document does not judge the NN representation or report a scientific success.

- `src/evaluate.py` reuses the R2 `Search.correct` method and frozen R4 `Model`/`Regularizer`, without modifying those sources. TRF uses the inherited exact analytic Jacobian, material bounds [.02,20], `x_scale='jac'`, exact trust-region solver, and all tolerances 1e-12. NN corrections have no smooth/TV penalty, at most 3 seconds and 8 nfev each, and stop at the first evaluated material meeting the .001 clean/.003 noisy physical data gate. Verification of that point uses the full nonlinear VIE, not the auxiliary current fit.
- The fixed candidate set is anchor, clipped half proposal, clipped full proposal, corrected half, corrected full. Feasible raw seeds need no correction and are retained as their corrected candidates. Both raw proposals are physically verified before correction begins. Finite candidates already produced survive a later budget or numerical failure. Failed/skipped attempts and absent predictions remain explicit.
- Online selection is the non-anchor feasible candidate closest in Euclidean material distance to the full original NN proposal. If none exists, a feasible anchor is the fallback. No feasible material means FAIL, with no fabricated top1. The original data + smooth/TV 1e-4 selection is saved separately. The independent controls use a strict data-only TRF and smooth/TV 1e-5 TRF, each within the same 10-second incremental budget, using strict TRF stopping without a physical-data-gate early stop. Physical qualification is evaluated afterward.
- Prospective operation guards enforce at most 100 LU and 6000 complex RHS. The 10 seconds includes incremental feature/inference preparation supplied by the prediction receipt, full-VIE verification, correction, auxiliary diagnostics, optional QP, and selection. Preparation missing a receipt marks accounting HOLD. Anchor construction is the separately reported shared sunk cost, not charged again to every arm. Reference-J setup and inference setup are separately identifiable costs. A noninterruptible library call can overshoot wall time; actual time and overshoot are preserved. The scorer reports the count actually inside all budgets separately.
- Auxiliary current data residual, constitutive defect, q-field defect (when q_aux exists), and verified full-VIE data residual have distinct fields. No auxiliary fit is accepted as physical material qualification.
- The optional secondary filter uses the full fixed anchor analytic J and a bounded ridge QP with lambda=(epsilon/||proposal displacement||)^2, epsilon=1e-4 clean/1e-3 noisy. No weak-spectrum truncation is performed. Its J, QP, and wall costs belong to the same arm budget. Main unfiltered results remain separate by run name.
- `src/score.py` first inventories all planned outputs, preserving NOT_RUN. It validates all sealed receipt/array sizes and modification times before opening any truth. It uses no hashes. Scoring uses amplitude-normalized RMSE, the historical reconstruction-only Otsu procedure, truth support alpha >= .5, and joint NRMSE <= .15 / IoU >= .7 / physical data qualification. Finite all-best-K and physically qualified best-K are offline diagnostics over the saved candidates, not unknown roots. Correction displacement and positive-material-gain retention are saved separately.
- The optional GT oracle is `j0 + (I-V48 V48*) (jGT-j0)`, followed by real rowwise material readout, explicit material clipping, and independent full nonlinear VIE verification. It is stored only under `evaluation/offline_oracle`, with separate offline operation/time costs. It never enters online candidates or ranking. Its auxiliary fit can be excellent while the material readout fails the physical data gate.
- Figures iterate all sealed observations and runs. Each observation uses one material color range shared by GT, GN, every raw output, and every selected output, including outliers. Missing/failed selections stay visible; figures paginate when needed. Cost and defect figures include every available row.

A fresh DEV integration completed with one available clean parent across all five one-epoch debug arms plus both controls; the second planned noisy observation lacked an anchor and is preserved as NOT_RUN. The public evaluation, prediction closure, GT scoring, offline receiver-null oracle and all-case figures completed. Figure readability and uniform color scale were visually checked. Known limitations: size/mtime closure is an audit inventory, not cryptographic tamper evidence. Wall time is checked between retained library operations; operations cannot be preempted. The 1e-5 control is a predeclared weak regularization comparator, not a guarantee of superior material reconstruction. Physical and material success remain separate until offline joint scoring.

Integration result detail (DEV-only, not generalization): five NN arms each retained five candidates, all physically feasible selections, incremental wall .051–.836 s, 3–5 LU, at most 108 complex RHS. Both controls retained the feasible GN anchor and used two LU. Joint gates failed due to IoU .536. The offline receiver-null oracle improved NRMSE from .14977 to .0089004, IoU to .86364, and had full-VIE data residual 3.813e-5; this establishes a representation ceiling on only this one debugging parent.

The prior DEV control numbers must not be cited as scientific ordinary-continuation results.

The scorer reports total/visible/null increment and current-error energies, auxiliary-current relative error versus jGT, error against P0(jGT−j0), and joins these with constitutive defect, raw material NRMSE, and selected material NRMSE. Only cached public V48 is used; no new SVD or physics is added by these diagnostics.

Execution partitioning: the public CLI supports one or two deterministic manifest-index partitions (`--partition-count 2 --partition-index 0/1`), optionally narrowed by explicit observation IDs. The two finite BLAS1 processes own disjoint observations and write separate partition manifests; no scientific per-arm budgets change. The pilot VAL watcher waits for every128-anchor/prediction cohort before starting both partitions, waits for both processes to finish, and then seals/scorers the complete plan. This introduces no service, framework, or GPU work.
