# Maxwell formula atlas maintenance

This directory is a living, source-traceable formula collection requested by the user.

- Before proposing expensive research, express the mechanism using existing formula IDs, identify fixed and changing operators, and derive the intended simplification.
- Each new formula needs dimensions/conventions, derivation or a precise primary source, assumptions, validity class, and computational consequence. Separate mathematical proof, implemented code, measured evidence and conjecture.
- Fixed coordinates do not require fixed coordinate values. Receiver-current spaces, material representations and local material sensitivities are different spaces.
- Keep current-to-material shared-source legality, original prior and the actual nonlinear Maxwell operator explicit.
- Exact posterior calibration is an optional independent objective; it must not block authorized material-possibility-volume, empirical coverage or material-risk work. Label approximate weights honestly.
- Do not launch numerical experiments merely to see an algebraic identity agree to machine precision. Use derivation/review for formulas; run experiments for scientific decisions, material/task performance and actual total cost.
- Preserve stable formula IDs; amend CHANGELOG.md when assumptions or formulas change. Never rewrite historical frozen experiment assets.
- Do not claim novelty for standard SVD, projection, Woodbury, coarea, conditional expectation or other established identities. State what is newly derived for the present model and what experimental claim remains open.
