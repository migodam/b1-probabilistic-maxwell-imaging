Lower bounds on peak dielectric contrast from finite-aperture scattering data

From the directory containing manuscript_ip.tex, run
    tectonic manuscript_ip.tex
or run pdflatex manuscript_ip.tex twice.

The accompanying files are iopjournal.cls, experiments.tex, appendix.tex,
references.tex and the six PDF figures under figures/. The empirical and
synthetic results in the paper refer to the numerical study described in
the article. The anonymous source bundle contains only the typesetting
inputs and this build instruction.
