# Native lossless compensation contract

Test whether the local constitutive equation improves the strict exclusion distance at fixed native scalar weights and unchanged lossless full-box data. The forward map uses all 9,216 independent real binary64-represented cell coefficients in [0,u], a fixed padded Green symbol, and a full complex 32-by-32 residual Frobenius ball with the inherited realized 2% radius and padding. The cohort consists of the six exposed frozen observations optfresh_01 through optfresh_06, each with suffix __pw360_32x32__pair2pct. Green symbol and arrays are read from the existing frozen files. No known material, material support, or truth scoring enters the test.

Primary arm u=1 uses scalar_grid_601, and the stable scalar optical weights give a paired sensitivity check. Positive regression uses stable scalar optical weights at u=.75. Higher cap uses scalar_grid_566 at u=1.25. These are native candidates already reported in v3. The control is the unchanged native inertia-tail expression. An optimistic upper control for compensation at fixed c sets the center charge to zero for the MY >= 0 variant. This optimistic control does not bound variants allowing negative center charge.

The first screen applies an all-pixel necessary condition for any nonnegative frame weights, at homogeneous material vectors drawn from a fixed 121-point cap grid. Follow-up applies pivoted QR to native endpoint negative rows, uses at most 2*nu and 4*nu pixels, and computes selected positive row Grams by projected solves. The endpoint sum is a sufficient frame, while infeasibility with it alone is scoped to that sufficient condition. The numerical search can try interior values and a center-aware source matrix on decisive cases. For an exclusion claim all relevant inequalities, the true complete center, inertia, source form, and noise comparison require outward checks on the 96² model.
