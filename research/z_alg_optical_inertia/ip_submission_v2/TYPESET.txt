Certifying peak dielectric contrast from finite-aperture scattering data

Double-anonymous LaTeX sources

The main file is manuscript_ip.tex. Keep iopjournal.cls alongside it
and keep the supplied PDF graphics in the figures directory.

Build with a TeX distribution that includes amsmath, amssymb, amsthm,
booktabs, microtype, graphicx, placeins, fancyhdr and hyperref.

    pdflatex -halt-on-error manuscript_ip.tex
    pdflatex -halt-on-error manuscript_ip.tex

Alternatively, a single invocation of Tectonic resolves the references.

    tectonic manuscript_ip.tex

The bibliography is included in manuscript_ip.tex.
