**Adaptive Sequences & Error Contract**
*Verified:* The shared-midpoint argument in [notes.md](../theory/paired.md) **applies to any causal one-at-a-time adaptive angle sequence** `(j_t, a_t)` with `sum |a_t|^2 <= E`: delivering `a_t m_{j_t}` keeps histories identical, bounding aggregate squared error by `E max_j kappa_j^2`.
*Error contract:* It requires a **joint worst-case Frobenius ball** (`<= r`) on stacked new data with `sqrt(E) max_j kappa_j <= r`—verified across all 15 angles on 11/12 `power_32` ([angle_bound_power_32.json](../beams/angle_bound_power_32.json); *exception:* row `310`, `1.064 > 1`, 13/15) and 2/2 `power_96` ([angle_bound_power_96.json](../beams/angle_bound_power_96.json), `0.919, 0.859`).
