# Distance between the two possible new readings

A material class, a fixed multiplier, a nonsingular state operator of negative index nu, and old measurements with a justified noise Gram upper bound give a Hermitian form Q on source commands and measured receivers. Suppose the bordered old block is invertible with exactly nu positive eigenvalues. Schur complementation and inertia additivity imply that every clean future reading for command a satisfies [a;y]*Q[a;y] <= 0. The factorization with the old block must use an error bound for the whole old matrix. Only the future *clean* response has a zero error row and column. A new measured vector can subsequently be tested at its own noise radius.

Let the two classes have Q_i = [[A_i,L_i*],[L_i,R_i]]. Let f(a) be the squared distance between their outer sets of clean readings. Introduce two nonnegative weights l_1 and l_2 and write
D = [[I+l_1 R_1,-I],[-I,I+l_2 R_2]], B = [l_1 L_1;l_2 L_2],
H = l_1 A_1 + l_2 A_2 - B* inv(D) B.
Whenever D is positive definite, completing the square in y_1 and y_2 gives f(a) >= a*H a. Thus one admissible weight pair and one command with a*H a > 4r^2 suffice for worst-case discrimination at error radius r. With command power a*W a = E, the best value for a given pair of weights is E times the largest generalized eigenvalue of (H,W). Perform the computation on the permitted source subspace. A common translation y_i <- y_i + F_mid a preserves distances and improves numerical accuracy.

Exactness for these *two quadratic outer sets*. Suppose for fixed a there are strictly feasible readings for both classes and some positive dual weights have D positive definite. Lift [y_1;y_2;1] times its adjoint to a positive semidefinite complex matrix. There are at most three active real affine equalities, including the last diagonal = 1. A rank-r extreme point must have r^2 <= 3: a Hermitian perturbation on its range has r^2 real parameters. The positive dual state makes feasible sublevel sets of the objective bounded; the bound follows by adding the two weighted constraints and completing the square, with off-diagonal entries controlled by positive semidefiniteness. These sets are closed, so an optimal extreme point exists (take an extreme point of the compact optimal face). Strict feasibility of the lifted primal follows by a sufficiently small positive diagonal addition in the state. Strong SDP duality and rank one prove exactness. If the optimal dual state is singular, mix it with the strictly feasible dual and take the limit. Under these assumptions for every nonzero admissible command, maximizing over a and the weights in either order gives the supremum of f at fixed power. For fixed absolute r the strict power threshold is 4r^2 divided by the supremal generalized eigenvalue, provided that value is positive. The power equality and threshold use homogeneity of the conditional forms for clean future readings. They do not apply unchanged to a noise radius that increases with power.

Failure diagnostics. If one nonzero v satisfies v*R_i v < 0 for both classes, every pair of fibres intersects for every fixed a: y=t v lies in both for sufficiently large t. A stronger, directly testable sufficient obstruction is a linear map T with [I;T]*Q_i[I;T] <= 0 for both i. Two distinct such maps, one per class, with weighted operator distance at most 2r/sqrt(E) likewise prevent a worst-case guarantee by these forms. These are obstructions to an outer approximation. In contrast, any two exact old-compatible material witnesses give the physical upper bound sqrt(f_phys(a)) <= ||(F_1-F_2)a||, where f_phys uses the material classes themselves. This proves necessary radiated energy for those witnesses to be distinguishable at every possible noisy reading. Strict prospective class separation and a particular new measurement rejecting a class are different statements.

Precedents. Exact rank reduction for small numbers of complex quadratic constraints and optimal coherent illumination for two known transfer matrices are established results. The research question is whether data-conditioned, full-wave material *classes* can exploit their combination, at realistic noise, after paying for field uncertainty.
