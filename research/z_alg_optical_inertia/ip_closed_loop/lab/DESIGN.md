# Transfer through measured receiving ports

Use all three Fresnel specimens (4, 6, 8 GHz) and all four Manitoba dielectric objects (3 to 6 GHz every 0.5 GHz). Same object at different frequencies is one specimen. Compare every unordered specimen pair per facility/frequency. This is a retrospective processing check at real measured ports. No extra physical acquisition and no loaded-error upper bound are available.

For Fresnel use the two common source directions 0 and 180 degrees and the two common measured receiving arcs; for Manitoba use all twelve even zero-index transmitters, twelve odd receivers. For each receiver block, design on one angular half and evaluate on the opposite half; swap the halves as a paired condition. Train only on the complex difference at design receivers. Compare the leading right singular vector with the best single transmitter on that same design subset. Source amplitudes have unit Euclidean norm in recorded coordinates. Store the score for the two known specimens; without a measured port-power Gram this is not a feed-efficiency experiment. Fresnel alternatives are per-specimen versus common empty reference. Manitoba alternatives are four independently tabulated metal-reference/empty choices. Record all cases including changes of sign in the advantage. Only results or derived scalar data are redistributed.
