# Can these complex readings come from any nonnegative first-Born image?

A lower bound on the peak of a full-wave material answers a different question from consistency with a linear scattering model. Here we allow the Born image to have any nonnegative contrast at all 9,216 cells, including peaks above the peaks tested with the nonlinear model. On six strong scatterers, one complex receiver weighting per array rules out this whole linear class on every one of the 96 paired fields. The lower distances are 40.23–61.56% of the measured field norm.

For any complex array weight Z, the Born signal generated by a real material has real pairing with Z equal to Σχᵢgᵢ, where gᵢ=Re tr(Z* S[:,i] B[i,:]). On a box the largest possible pairing is Σuᵢ(gᵢ)₊. On the entire nonnegative cone it is zero if every gᵢ≤0. Subtract this support value from Re tr(Z*Y), divide by ||Z||_F, and retain the positive part. Cauchy–Schwarz proves a lower bound on the distance from Y to the whole class. This is ordinary convex separation. The physical choice of Z uses the instrument geometry.

Solve S*C≈B using receiver singular values above 1% of the largest. Set qᵢ=Σ(S*C)ᵢⱼ conjugate(Bᵢⱼ). When Re qᵢ>0 at every cell, choose η≥max(0,maxᵢ −Im qᵢ/Re qᵢ) and Z=(i−η)C. Then gᵢ=−Im qᵢ−η Re qᵢ≤0. We check these signs on the actual complex operators, with the receiver mask appropriate to each source. In the ideal case S*C=B, the unrotated imaginary pairing reads the extinction Im tr(B*J). For the lossless nonlinear equations this equals tr(J* Im_H(G) J), while for a real Born current J=diag(χ)B it vanishes. Finite sampling can make this field matching poor. Checking all cell signs gives the desired half-space even when the match is approximate. A nonpositive observed projection leaves the question open.

The eight synthetic arrays needed 0.2–0.8 s each to compute and verify their reusable weights. A new field takes about 0.5 ms for an outward-rounded readout. A stronger data-adapted dual direction costs more and rules out the cone for the 0.04-contrast disks at both noise levels when the fixed readout vanishes. The ultraweak full-wave ellipse and the exactly Born-generated control both admit explicit fits inside their balls.

With the actual chamber masks, the fixed weights exceed two empirical mismatch scales for all three 4 GHz Fresnel targets and for 25/28 Manitoba object–frequency pairs. These distances concern real nonnegative Born images in the calibrated two-dimensional instrument model. Loss, antenna modeling and loaded-target error affect their physical interpretation. Positive comparative margins do not provide an upper bound on all these effects.

## 中文判读

“任意摆放峰值小于 0.5 的全波材料都无法拟合”和“任意摆放任何强度的非负 Born 材料都无法拟合”是两个独立问题。对后者，只需把每个接收通道乘以预先用天线几何算好的复数再求和。若给任何一个像素放置正对比度都只能把结果往一个方向推，而实测读数越过相反方向的噪声界，整个线性模型就被排除了。六件强散射物体上该运算均有效。换成峰值只有 0.04 的双圆盘时，固定读数无法判定，需要针对观测选更合适的方向。这说明该办法能便宜地辨别某些场景何时需要多次散射，也给出了它会失灵的实际例子。
