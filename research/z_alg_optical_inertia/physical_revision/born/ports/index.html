暗室复核

按每个源的实际接收位置建立权重。对每组 \(g\)，以 1% 相对奇异值截断从 \(S_g\) 和空场校准的 \(B_g\) 得到权 \(C_g\)。在全部源组上先逐像素加总 \(Q_i=\sum_g\sum_t(S_g^*C_g)_{it}\overline{(B_g)_{it}}\)，再按原规则选择共同系数 \(\eta=\max(0,\max_i-\operatorname{Im}Q_i/\operatorname{Re}Q_i)+2\times10^{-8}\)，令 \(Z_g=(i-\eta)C_g\)。这一步只用几何和空场参考。区间核验每个像素的合并实内积非正，因而对所有共享的非负实材料成立。在线读数是各组 \(\operatorname{Re}\langle Z_g,Y_g\rangle_F\) 求和并除以共同的权重范数。缺测端口不参与，也不补零来推算共同照明。

这些读数检验的是选定校准和离散模型内的无损非负 Born 类。经验标尺由原校准对照取得，尚无覆盖真实测量的统一校准和三维模型误差上界。Manitoba 尼龙另有小损耗，实测偏差可能包含线性有损响应。因而上述计数是对误差预算的条件比较，不能单凭它们判定暗室对象必须发生可分辨的多次散射。

Reproduction

Run the following from the project root with NumPy, SciPy, python-flint and threadpoolctl. The replay reads the original Manitoba private calibrated port archive and original Fresnel raw and derived records. The measured replay reads local caches built from the collectors’ data. Its weights depend on the empty-field calibration and stay with those local files. The public archive includes the derived tables and reconstruction entry points. Data access and local preparation

python -B research/z_alg_optical_inertia/physical_revision/born/ports/replay.py

The single-threaded replay took 22.39 s wall and 22.00 s CPU. The original synthetic geometry and six weak readouts took less than 0.2 s. All Manitoba geometries and readouts took 0.39 s wall. Fresnel took 21.83 s wall including reading the original files, repeating the empty-field calibration, forming nine masked weights and checking the pixel signs over 3,600 cells.
