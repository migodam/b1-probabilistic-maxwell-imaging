# How many independent incident waves reach the material?

A 400 MHz array with 24 equally spaced incident directions predicts the clean response at 32 directions to within 0.0082–0.1423% for all six shapes. The error ranges from 2.63 to 13.42% with 16 directions and from 45.90 to 75.08% with ten. Each comparison resamples an independently computed full nonlinear response and restores identical total incident fluence. Neither the angular fit nor the reference supports enter the online peak bound. The 24-to-32 change in the number of excluded objects is zero at the tested contrasts and noise levels. Its meaning depends on the measurement tolerance.

For each material and a common receiver set, write the complex scattered field as a function of the incident angle. If the contrast vanishes outside a support A, the response is a fixed linear operator on the incident field restricted to A, even with full multiple scattering. Jacobi–Anger expansion bounds its harmonic of order l by the norm of this operator times the norm of the vector of Bessel functions J_l(k|x_i|) on A. This explains the rapid decay outside the angular orders comparable to the electrical radius. A large resolvent can amplify the tail. The imaging square uses its *circumscribed* radius a=W/√2. The six reference supports fit in circles with 2ka between 11.89 and 14.86, while the entire search square has 2ka=23.70. Eight more transmit angles resolve little new field in these cases. The incident-field interpolation error on the whole square is still 4.87% at 24 angles.

For global sector weight μ>0 and nonnegative normalized optical weight t, set H=Re_H G, R=Im_H G and A=I+tR+S* S/μ. For A positive definite, Sylvester's law gives n_-(K)=#{eigenvalues of A^(-1/2) H A^(-1/2) above 1/u}. The plain reactive count uses A=I. The finite positive receiver update removes at most rank(S) negative directions. On the square the numerical plain counts are 19, 21, 25, 36 and 45 as u increases from 0.75 through 1, 1.25, 1.5 and 2. Interval rank bounds on both sides prove equality at all five thresholds on the full grid. For comparison, on a periodic translation-invariant model away from a reciprocal-lattice pole the Fourier multiplier k²/(|ξ|²−k²) predicts that k<|ξ|<k√(1+u) can contribute. The annular lattice area divided by the frequency cell area is |Ω|k²u/(4π). This calculation motivates an electrical-area scale. The actual outgoing finite-domain counts and the angular bandwidth require distinct computations. Neither a Weyl asymptotic for the open scatterer nor a saturation threshold for all measurements follows.

An exact check of source redundancy uses any rectangular right isometry U* with UU*=I. Replacing B,Y by BU,YU preserves incident energy and changes H_Y to U*H_YU. The positive spectrum and thus the distance bound are unchanged. For approximate rank q≤ν, interlacing gives d²≤tr[(Π H_Y Π)_+] for any orthogonal projection Π of codimension q on source space (positive part in ran Π). In particular d²≤||YΠ||_F²+||(-K^(-1))_+^(1/2)LΠ||_F². This follows by deleting the nonpositive contribution to the compressed source matrix. It explains how redundancy or a small source tail can suppress the bound even when s>ν. Weak positive and negative gaps of K affect this estimate. Counting singular values of B alone cannot decide the measured margin.

On the 24² ring the extent is specified in wavelength units (width 0.96λ); its data do not fix a physical frequency in hertz. At u=5 the weighted count is four for 12 line sources. The verified joint distance 0.014695 exceeds radius 0.010517. On the 400 MHz square, 32 receivers and 32 incident directions leave a lower bound of only 0.3011r with the strongest replayed u=1 profile, although the stable profile has just thirteen negative current directions. The missing positive response after those directions have been removed is observable directly.

In the Manitoba chamber the same 0.14 m field of view spans 1.40 to 2.80 wavelengths. With a fixed dimensionless receiver weight, the computed bare count at u=1 increases 9,11,13,15,17,19,21 and the receiver-weighted count 0,0,3,8,12,15,20 between 3 and 6 GHz in half-GHz increments. The two measured reciprocal groups each provide 12 sources. At 5 GHz and above this weighting gives an empty sum. Other profiles and other error scales can yield different outcomes. The E-shaped polyethylene data, in particular, have a positive conditional margin at 6 GHz after losing it at 5.5 GHz. Comparisons of object and calibration explain why there is no empirical monotone frequency law for all loaded targets.

同样的总入射能量，先从 10 束增加到 24 束，六件物体里能确定峰值超过 0.75 的物体增多。24 束继续加到 32 束，六件物体的结果全部持平。把前 24 束的全波散射场插值到 32 个实际计算的入射方向，误差最高 0.143%，已经明显小于 1% 的噪声。在 16 束时误差最高仍有 13.42%。这里解释的是这六件物体在这个孔径和误差水平下的现象。

峰值门槛升到 1 时，即使发射和接收各有 32 个角度，且负方向只有 13 个，场差下界仍只有误差半径的 0.301 倍。读数能不能排除一类材料，取决于剩下的照明方向有多大的实测场差。较小环达到峰值 5 和加拿大暗室随频率上升的退化，也可以用电尺寸和剩余源方向作定量比较。暗室有反弹的个例，标定误差与材料本身都参与决定最后余量。
